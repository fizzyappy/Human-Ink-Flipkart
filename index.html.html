How a note gets sealed
01

Write it the way you always have.

Real ink, any paper. An accelerometer and a gyroscope in the pen track every movement of the nib, so each stroke is also saved as a digital copy.

Paper and digital, in one go
02

It checks for a person, not a printer.

A grip sensor feels your fingers. A motion sensor picks up the 8 to 12 Hz tremor every human hand has. Robot pens and traced copies don't have it.

Grip · tremor · pressure
03

Tick the circle. That's the seal.

The secure chip inside the pen signs the page: written by a person, how long it took, how many corrections. The light in the end cap glows amber.

Signed inside the pen
04

Click the end. Your phone sends it.

No photo, no scan. The pen saved every stroke as you wrote. Click the end cap and that copy goes to your phone over Bluetooth, then to Arjun on your usual chat app, sealed. The paper stays with you.

Pen → your phone → Arjun
05

Their assistant can't flatten it.

When Arjun's AI meets a sealed note, it shows "A person wrote this" and your handwriting in full, never a summary. Anyone can check the seal with a free verifier.

Shown whole, never summarised
06

And the card asks for an answer.

Every Ink Card has a tear-off half. Arjun writes back by hand, seals it, and sends it back the same way.

The Ink Keyboard

It feels how you press, not just what.

For the notes you type. Every keycap feels your fingertip, and magnetic switches measure how hard each key goes down. A hand never presses two keys the same way, so the amber Seal key signs only when touch, pressure and timing all vary like a person's.

How typing gets sealed

Typed, not pasted. Then sealed with one key.

01TypeEach press is felt, weighed and timed to 1 ms. A person's touch, pressure and rhythm vary in a way scripts and robot fingers rarely fake.
02Paste is noticedThe keyboard keeps a signed log of every key. Text that doesn't match it can't be sealed as typed.
03Press SealThe amber key signs the text and types a short seal link after it.
04Send anywhereEmail, chat or a doc. The link travels with the words.
05Edits break itChange one word after sealing and the fingerprint won't match, so the seal shows nothing.

Questions

Before you buy.

Does the seal prove I thought of the words?

It proves a human hand wrote them with this pen, with the timing and corrections. If you copy out words someone else wrote, the seal says "handwritten", not "original".

What does the person receiving it need?

Nothing. Their assistant or a free public verifier shows the seal. If nothing on their side supports it, they still get your note, just without the badge.

Can I write on any paper?

Yes. The motion sensors track the pen itself, not the paper, so any paper works. Ink Cards add a seal circle to tick and a tear-off reply half.

Can I return an engraved pen?

Engraved pens can only be returned if they're faulty. The 3D preview shows your engraving before you add it, so check the spelling there.

Is this a real product?

Not yet. This is a concept prototype. Nothing is charged and nothing ships.
